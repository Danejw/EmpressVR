# Who We Are

We are an **Enterprise Co-operative**, owned and operated by our members, and every member gets a vote in how we are run. But more than that, we’re a **Global Community** of individuals from diverse backgrounds who are exploring careers as virtual reality developers, immersive storytellers, creative technologists, sound designers, and 3D modelers among many others. We are exploring the innovative intersection between technology and traditional art forms, to **create emotionally complex and technically experimental works**, to test the limits of our own imaginations, and **surprise and delight audiences** while working to make the world a better and more equitable place by fulfilling on our primary objective:

**To Amplify Diverse Voices in Immersive Storytelling.**
