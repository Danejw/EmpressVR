- 1\. Spatial Audio System Demo - Thawney



















Robin Thorn (they/them), working as Thawney, is a Bristol-based creative technologist, musician and designer exploring spatial audio, haptics and accessible music technology. Their Spatial Audio System uses custom omnidirectional loudspeakers, amplification and multichannel playback to create shared immersive listening environments that audiences can move through.



Alongside this, Robin develops haptics systems that translate sound and movement into physical vibration, extending immersive audio beyond hearing alone. Their work combines electronics, programming, speaker design and interaction design, using purpose-built hardware to explore

how sound can be felt, shared and experienced differently across space through bodies, rooms, movement and community.

- 3\. Panopticon - Martina Klánová



















Panopticon is a surreal psychological experience exploring the metaphor of the mind as a prison. Set inside a tall structure, the film draws the viewer into a series of dimly lit and oddly shaped cells – each inhabited by symbolic objects, animated fragments of memory, desire, fear, and control. A cigarette, a wallet, a pair of scissors, a bone… Each cell holds a fragment of a larger subconscious narrative.



Filmed as a 360° video in miniature cardboard sets and animated using stop-motion techniques, Panopticon combines handcrafted German-expressionism-esque aesthetics with digital compositing to create an unsettling and immersive space. The VR format places the viewer in position of quiet authority – or surveillance – within the prison’s centre.

- 4\. Metamorphic Avatars - Conrad Wilson



















Metamorphic Avatars is a short VR experience that allows users to test out various avatar types. Have a mess around in the mirror and try out a range of characters and animals, from a snake to… Kirby? The magic under the hood is part of my personal ongoing VR development efforts and is but a

snapshot of a larger collection of systems the need proper public testing. The ultimate goal is to support people with varying body measurements to fluidly rescale into any avatar of their choosing, no matter how non-standard or non-humanoid they may be.

- 6\. Beyond the Screen - Tanish Raorane, Alex Thompson, Dev Raju



















**Beyond the Screen: Redefining XR Through Haptics and Spatial Sound**



This project explores 3DoF force-feedback haptics and spatial audio via the Senmag 9D Stylus to construct non- visual VR experiences, advancing inclusive design and spatial accessibility. Set in an abandoned hospital, the high-fidelity Hospital Maze simulation studies how Blind and Visually

Impaired (BVI) users navigate complex environments using non-visual percepts. Operating as a virtual haptic cane, the stylus allows users to physically feel walls, doors, and furniture textures. Developed in Unity with custom safety parameters to prevent harsh force feedback on wall impacts,

the project investigates how touch and sound can guide intuitive spatial navigation when vision is removed.



**Credits**



Creators : **Tanish Raorane, Alex Thompson, Dev Raju**



Mentor : **Dr. Kyle Keane**

- 7\. Forkbeard Fantasy: An Immersive Reinterpretation - Harry Clarke, Bhuvan Srinivas Chandrababu



















Forkbeard Fantasy: An Immersive Reinterpretation is a 15- minute seated VR experience rebuilding the archive of Forkbeard Fantasy, a British company that combined theatre, film, animation and puppetry until it closed in 2012 after its arts funding was withdrawn. Developed with the University of Bristol Theatre Collection, the piece uses photogrammetry and digital reconstruction to bring

characters, props and footage from the archive back into motion. The audience moves between three theatrical perspectives in front of the stage, backstage, and on stage as a decaying archive performs itself around them. A physical exhibition of original archive material surrounds the

headsets.

- 8\. RouteSetter - Javier Rodríguez Lavandeira

























This project is a VR route-setting tool for indoor climbing gyms, designed to make the creation and management of climbing routes more intuitive and immersive. It allows users to create, modify, and climb routes within a virtual climbing gym environment, with the system remembering previously created routes through persistent session management. The tool enables climbing holds and volumes to be positioned directly onto virtual walls, providing an interactive alternative to traditional route-setting methods. By combining immersive VR interaction with persistent route

storage, the project explores how virtual reality can support both route creation and climbing practice in a virtual climbing environment.



**Credits**



**Javier Rodríguez Lavandeira**

- 11\. Egyptian Ascent: Through the Abyss - Ahmed T Fathy, Badr El Sherwani



















Step into a grand vertical expanse where time unwinds in reverse as you drop down a magnificent historical abyss. Instead of flying forward, you experience a controlled descent through three distinct architectural eras: starting among the intricate patterns of the Islamic era, passing through the imposing columns of the Greco-Roman period, and descending into the monumental carved stone of Ancient Egypt. By leveraging natural gravity and vertical parallax, this immersive descent mirrors an archaeological excavation where physical strata rise past your field of view, turning a continuous plunge into the depths of history into an awe-inspiring ascent of cultural understanding.



**Credits**



**Ahmed T Fathy** – Associate Professor in Digital Media Design & Head of the Game Design Track, School of Creative Arts, University of Hertfordshire hosted by Global Academic Foundation

- 12\. Imprints - Beth Lewis

























The development stages of drawing have remained the same throughout human existence. The art of our ancestors sings through the first drawings of our children, all of them following the same trajectory for 20,000 years: handprints to scribbles to spiral to circles to radial forms to perpendicular lines to human and animal representative forms. Imprints invites you to return to crafting these fundamental structures, and to continue the visual conversation with our ancestors.



**Credits**



Created by **Beth Lewis**



Inspired by **'First Drawings: Genesis of Visual Thinking' by Sylvia Fein**
