# Fireside

[Projects](https://www.empressvr.com/projects)

By the Eunoia Collab

For the first annual DeepWell Digital Therapeutics May Day Mental Health Game Jam, a bunch of friends and I joined forces to be the Eunoia Collab and make a Virtual Reality social experience. This is the outcome!

The lighting, sound, and several elements of the game were deliberately chosen to signal for safety and connection.
