## **Empress Immersive -**  **Membership Terms & Conditions**

_Last updated: 1st July 2025_

Welcome to **Empress Immersive LTD**, a UK-based cooperative business that is member-owned and operated. We’re focused on advancing the field of immersive art and storytelling. By becoming a member, you are entering into a community founded on cooperation, creativity, and shared purpose. These Terms and Conditions explain what membership entails, including your rights, responsibilities, and the benefits of co-ownership.

## **About Our Co-operative**

Empress Immersive is a UK-registered **Enterprise Co-operative**, guided by the **7 Co-operative Principles** and **10 Co-operative Values** stewarded globally by the **International Co-operative Alliance**. We are:

> "An autonomous association of persons united voluntarily to meet their common economic, social and cultural needs and aspirations through a jointly-owned and democratically-controlled enterprise."

Our members co-own this co-operative. Each member has a voice — and a vote — in how we are governed and grown. [Learn more about the International Cooperative Values and Principles here.](https://ica.coop/en/cooperatives/cooperative-identity)

## **Eligibility for Membership**

To become a member, you must:

- Be at least 18 years old

_(We aim to include younger members aged 16+ in the future with appropriate safeguarding. Non-voting youth membership is under consideration.)_

- Have a home address in your country of tax residency

- Be willing to have your identity verified (for UK legal compliance)

- Be willing to pay the fees based on your chosen membership level

- Be willing to commit to the rights and responsibilities outlined in your chosen membership level

- Be an **immersive art/storytelling enthusiast or creator**, or aspire to become one. This includes, but is not limited to:

  - Physical installation designers

  - Interactive/immersive theatre artists

  - VR/AR/MR creators

  - Escape room or LARP designers

  - Sound, lighting, and 3D media artists

  - Narrative technologists and creative coders

  - Be a business engaged in any of the above activities

## **Types of Membership**

We offer three membership types to serve our different stakeholder groups. All members pay a **£1 one-time joining fee** and receive a **Co-operative Share Certificate** within 4 weeks of acceptance.

### **1\. Supporter Member**

**For:** Individuals or organisations who support our mission but do not actively use our services.

**Cost:** £1 one-time joining fee

**Benefits:**

- Vote in **Quarterly Ordinary General Meetings**

- Vote in **Annual Board Member Elections**

- Run for one of **3 Board Member Positions**


**Responsibilities:**

- Contribute **2–4 hours/week** of cooperative project work (in an area of interest or “learn by doing” opportunity)

- Attend at least **5 quarterly meetings per year**

- Develop working knowledge of our **governing articles** and the **global immersive industry**


### **2\. User Member**

**For:** Individuals or organisations who actively use our services (including creators and audience members)

**Cost:**

- £1 one-time joining fee

- Sliding scale **quarterly subscription** (£13–£52) based on need


**Benefits:**

- All Supporter Member benefits

- **Discounts** on:

  - Educational programs

  - Events, exhibitions, and immersive productions

**Responsibilities:**

Same as Supporter Members.

### **3\. Employee Member**

**For:** People employed by Empress Immersive who voluntarily choose to become co-op members.

_(Currently no employees, but this framework is in place for future hires.)_

**Cost:** £1 one-time joining fee

**Benefits & Responsibilities:** Same as User Members

## **Meetings & Participation**

Members have the right and responsibility to participate in our democratic process. Meetings include:

### **Ordinary General Meetings (OGMs)**

- **Quarterly:** January, April, July, October

- **Open to all members**

- **30 mins**

- Includes:

  - Board and finance updates

  - Proposals and votes from members and board

  - Discussion of collective goals

### **Annual General Meeting (AGM)**

- **January**

- **Board elections**

- **Voting on annual goals and co-op-wide projects**


### **Monthly Board Meetings (MBMs)**

- **Second Monday of each month**

- **Board members only required; all other members welcome**

- Members may submit agenda items 2 weeks in advance


## **Member Rights**

All members are entitled to:

- One vote at Annual General Meetings and Ordinary General Meetings

- Stand for election to the Board (if eligible)

- Participate in decision-making and co-op projects

- Be treated equitably and with respect

- Access cooperative resources, training, and discounts (as applicable)


## **Member Responsibilities**

All members are expected to:

- Actively contribute **2–4 hours/week** toward co-operative goals

- Respond to communication and calls to action

- Attend and vote in general meetings

- Uphold co-operative values and the governing documents

- Respect community guidelines and fellow members


## **Board Member Eligibility**

To stand for a Board Member position, you must:

- Be an active Empress Immersive member for **at least one quarter**

- Have attended **at least 2 OGMs**

- **Not** be:

  - Under 16 years of age

  - A disqualified director

  - An undischarged bankrupt

  - An auditor of the cooperative

## **Membership Termination**

Membership may be terminated if:

- A member voluntarily withdraws in writing

- A member fails to meet participation requirements without communication

- There is a serious breach of co-operative values or governing rules

(Disputes will follow a fair internal review process.)
