## John Howard

* * *

John Howard brings over a decade of robust software development experience, complemented by two years in an instructional role, where he taught Unity and C# development. His expertise spans various programming languages and platforms, including Perl, C++, C#, Unity, and SQL, with proficiency on systems such as Windows, Linux, Arduino, WebGL, Quest, and Android.

He holds dual Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics from Indiana University and is also a certified Unity developer.
