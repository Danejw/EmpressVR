# Sunlink

[Projects](https://www.empressvr.com/projects)

Originally developed as a submission to the [AWE XR Climate Challenge](https://auggies.awexr.com/gallery/BLgzmenL/XjjAKqqJ?search=058968f0edf9ec65-61), Sunlink is a groundbreaking virtual reality experience designed to inspire action and foster awareness about climate change. In this immersive journey, you will explore the power of microbial fuel cells (MFCs) and their integration with greenhouses to create sustainable, closed-loop systems.

Sunlink's mission is to educate users about the impact of individual choices on the environment, with a focus on the next generation who will face the challenges posed by a changing climate. Dive into the captivating world of MFCs, where organic waste is transformed into electricity and valuable resources, powering greenhouse systems and nurturing plants in an eco-friendly cycle.
