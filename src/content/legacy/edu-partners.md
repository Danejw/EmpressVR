## What We Offer

We offer a range of services to meet the needs of every client. Have something else in mind? We'd be happy to work with you to create a custom quote.

- Technical Education



















Our Creative Technologists bring hands-on experience across core XR tools and workflows, including Unity with C# development, animation, and lighting; Blender for 3D modelling, texturing, and performance-conscious asset creation; spatial audio production in Reaper; and rapid prototyping using Meta SDKs. As the industry evolves, we emphasise not just technical proficiency but adaptability—teaching resilient, structured problem-solving so teams can navigate setbacks, maintain momentum, and consistently deliver.

- Bespoke XR Lecture Series



















We design and deliver bespoke XR lecture series tailored to university needs, combining deep expertise in immersive technologies with proven teaching practice. Our team supports both educators and students through hands-on instruction in tools like Unity and Blender, alongside curriculum design, workflow integration, and real-world application of XR across disciplines.

- 1:1 XR Tutoring - All Levels



















We offer tailored 1:1 XR tutoring for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners refining production workflows. Sessions combine hands-on guidance in tools like Unity and Blender with clear explanations, practical exercises, and focused problem-solving. Our approach builds both technical capability and independent thinking, so learners can confidently develop, iterate, and complete their own immersive projects with a growing sense of XR fluency.

- Immersive Storytelling Workshops



















We deliver immersive storytelling workshops focused on “show, don’t tell” through interactive, spatial design. Participants learn to craft story worlds for story doing and story living, developing narrative maps, meaningful objects, and environmental cues—lighting, sound, and space—that guide audience experience across VR, immersive theatre, festivals, LARP, and escape rooms.

- Corporate Virtual Reality Demos



















We deliver tailored corporate VR demos designed to help leaders and creative teams confidently explore immersive technology. Sessions introduce VR etiquette, environments, and practical applications—from collaboration with international clients to prototyping for design and architecture. Each experience is carefully planned, fully guided, and can support strategic exploration or engaging team-building, cultivating lasting VR fluency.

- Custom Project - Co-created to fit your needs



















We offer fully bespoke XR projects, co-created to align with your specific goals, audience, and context. From early concept development through to delivery, we collaborate closely to design immersive experiences, training programmes, or prototypes that are practical, innovative, and outcome-focused—adapting our approach to meet your needs with clarity and precision.
