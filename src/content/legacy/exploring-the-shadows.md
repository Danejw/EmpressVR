_Exploring the Shadows_ is an immersive Virtual Reality (VR) experience designed by [Dominique Vyborna](https://www.empressvr.com/dominique-vyborna) to educate and support individuals with PTSD by blending digital therapeutics with an emotionally compelling, fantastical narrative. The project provides a safe, engaging space for users to learn about trauma symptoms, causes, and healing modalities while restoring a sense of agency through interactive storytelling.

This phase of the _Exploring the Shadows_ project development was made possible through the support of Culture West, with funding **from the West of England Combined Authority and Arts Council England.** From November 2024 to June 2025, we have been focused on R&D for a trauma-informed virtual environment design with interactive elements, which would provide a comfortable environment option for people of many diverse backgrounds.

Through community-led focus groups, we are committed that the virtual environments feel safe and welcoming to people from any background, including individuals from the global majority, women, LGBTQ+ communities, and neurodiverse people.

**If you missed the focus groups, but would still like to contribute, please take this** [**Safe Places Survey**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf0Jofplah2ofRUryOeXJ4JyTUu6ImzKCfzWCgkMw1ayeCd0Q/viewform?usp=dialog) **!**

The experience guides users through three pathways—each representing a different aspect of trauma education—before converging in a peaceful, multiplayer space designed for reflection and support. By integrating passive digital therapeutics, such as sound therapy, guided breathing, and grounding exercises, _Exploring the Shadows_ helps users navigate trauma education in a way that is both accessible and empowering.

This project aims to bridge the gap between trauma onset and formal therapy, offering a unique, innovative approach to mental health care.

**_This project is current in it’s fourth prototyping iteration, and we are actively seeking research funding for the co-design and testing of digital therapeutic elements to include in the final production._**
