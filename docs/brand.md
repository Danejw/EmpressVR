# EmpressVR brand reference

Based on Empress Immersive Branding & Marketing Guide 2025 (provided by the client).

- Primary: white `#FFFFFF`, whitish blue `#DADDEC`, dark navy `#171D3A`
- Secondary: pale magenta `#ED68E7`, pale green `#8DF382`, hot orange `#F36000`
- Accent: violet `#7700FF`, lime yellow `#CCFF00`, dark green `#14CC00`
- Website type: Young Serif headings, Adelle Sans body (Fira Sans suggested free alternative)
- Visual identity: cosmic crown logo; use licensed client-supplied brand art in `public/` when available.

This scaffold uses system type fallbacks until licensed fonts and original logo assets are committed. Avoid approximating the client's logo in permanent assets.
